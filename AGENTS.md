# Agent Guidelines - acr-logos

## Brand Identity & Vector Asset Discipline
1. **Vector-First Standard**: Maintain source vector assets in `svg/` and `illustrator/` alongside high-res raster exports in `png/`.
2. **Color Palette Invariant**: Adhere strictly to the luminous cyan (`#38bdf8`), indigo (`#6366f1`), and emerald (`#34d399`) brand scale.
